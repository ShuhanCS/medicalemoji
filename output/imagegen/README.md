## Selected concept

`white-blood-cell-gpt-image-2-concept-v2.png` is the selected transparent concept. It was generated from the
base prompt, refined once to make the connected three-lobed nucleus smaller and more biological, and converted
from a flat chroma-key background to alpha.

The concept established the organic folded membrane, pale body, and asymmetric S-shaped nucleus used in the
v1.17.0 artwork. The exact proposal assets were then redrawn as editable SVG geometry and exported
deterministically at 18x18 and 72x72 in colour and true black and white. The GPT Image 2 raster is not embedded
in the reviewer-facing proposal PDF.
