## 2026 format and evidence implications

The current guidance requires one public PDF per emoji, example images in color and true black-and-white at
18x18 and 72x72 near the top of the first page, a clear image-rights statement, every inclusion and exclusion
factor, and screenshots from all five required frequency sources. Missing screenshots keep a packet in draft
status even when its narrative and artwork are otherwise complete.
