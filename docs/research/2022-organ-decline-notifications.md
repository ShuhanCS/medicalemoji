The three messages use the same form language. They thank the submitter, state that the proposal was not

## What the current rule says

The current proposal guidelines say: “Emoji declined within the last four years are not eligible for
re-review.” The FAQ says declined proposals cannot be resubmitted “during that period.” The status key says
declined emoji are not eligible to be resubmitted for four years.

- https://www.unicode.org/emoji/proposals.html
- https://www.unicode.org/faq/emoji_submission.html
- https://www.unicode.org/emoji/emoji-proposals-status.html

None of those pages defines whether the period begins on the submission date, internal decision date,
notification date, annual status-sheet update, or another date. None states whether the rule applies
retroactively to proposals declined before the rule was introduced.

## Why eligibility is unresolved rather than disproved

Unicode's March 20, 2024 intake announcement labels the four-year restriction as a new change:

https://blog.unicode.org/2024/03/emoji-submissions-intake-process-re.html

The current public status sheet also lists April 2024 submissions for Spine, Intestines, and ECG even though
the same concepts have late-2020 declined submissions. Every possible decline date for those 2020 proposals
was less than four years before April 2024. The project received a November 13, 2024 internal update saying
the 2024 maze, intestines, ECG, and spine submissions were rejected, which is consistent with those
submissions entering the annual review cycle rather than being blocked at upload.

This record does not prove that Kidney, Liver, or Stomach is eligible in July 2026. It does show that a
categorical “ineligible” conclusion is not supported by the public rule text or by Unicode's visible 2024
handling.

## Narrow question for Unicode

> For Kidney, Liver, and Stomach proposals declined by notices dated November 4, 2022, are renewed proposals
> eligible in the intake window ending July 31, 2026? If the answer depends on the four-year restriction,
> which event starts the period, and does the restriction announced as new in March 2024 apply to decisions
> issued in 2022?

This is a procedural eligibility question, not a request for favorable review.
