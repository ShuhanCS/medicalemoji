The Medical Emoji Project

A Universal Language
for Health

Bringing medicine to the emoji keyboard on every phone — clearer health communication across languages, literacy levels, and devices.
The Gap
3,600+ emoji exist.
Almost none represent medicine.

Billions of people interact with emoji daily — yet the human body, conditions, and tools of healthcare are barely there.

🫀🫁

It already works.

The project helped bring the anatomical heart and lungs into Unicode in 2021 — now on billions of devices worldwide, with the effort documented in JAMA.
