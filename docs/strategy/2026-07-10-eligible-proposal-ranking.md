# 2026 re-eligible Medical Emoji slate: decision brief

Date: 2026-07-10
Audience: Shuhan He and Microsoft internal reviewers
Decision: Choose one lead concept for the 2026-07-31 Unicode intake and, at most, one fallback.

## Recommendation

**Advance CT Scan to Microsoft review now. Make Weight Scale the challenger after a manual Google Trends recapture. Keep Blood Bag as the packet-complete fallback. Do not file the full eight-concept slate.**

All nine concepts below are clearly outside the four-year resubmission period. That removes a procedural bar but does not improve the underlying selection odds of a resubmission. A focused Microsoft review of one lead concept is easier to defend against Unicode's `Open-ended` exclusion factor and creates a credible request for product, design, and legal attention.

## Evidence interpretation

The historical Search result counts are directionally useful but noisy and should not be treated as precise popularity measurements. Current Google Trends comparisons matter more for the six evidence-gated drafts. CT Scan's Google Books curve is the strongest of these nine and is visibly of the same order as `elephant`; the other eight remain substantially below it in recent published-book usage.

Three packets have all five required categories today: CT Scan, Blood Bag, and Pill Box. Their Search, Video, and Trends captures date to 2020 and should be refreshed if Google permits a clean manual session, but they are labeled accurately and contain the required comparator. Weight Scale, White Blood Cell, Inhaler, IV Bag, Leg Cast, and Pill Pack are not ready to file until the specified Video or Trends charts are captured.

## Portfolio conflicts to resolve

- **Blood Bag versus IV Bag:** preserve Blood Bag's droplet emblem and IV Bag's unmarked clear fluid. File no more than one unless independent evidence demonstrates that both remain distinct at 18x18 and in monochrome.
- **Pill Box versus Pill Pack:** Pill Box represents reusable organization; Pill Pack represents sealed packaging. Their medication-management uses overlap enough that filing both would weaken the open-ended answer.
- **CT Scan versus X-Ray:** lead with appointment, scanner, cross-sectional imaging, and procedure meanings. Do not argue that every imaging modality deserves an emoji.
- **Weight Scale versus Balance Scale:** lead with the floor platform and the verbs `weigh` and `track`; avoid justice or fairness language, which belongs to Balance Scale.
- **Leg Cast and modifiers:** if advanced, confirm whether it belongs in People & Body and must support the standard skin-tone modifiers.
- **Inhaler and the 2018 record:** lead with the concrete actions missing from the earlier proposal - carry, find, use, forget, refill, and pack - and do not rely on disease prevalence or medication-set completeness.

## What to ask Microsoft for

1. A Windows/Segoe UI Emoji product-owner choice between CT Scan and a Trends-complete Weight Scale proposal.
2. A Fluent Emoji or Windows design review of the lead paradigm at 18x18 and in black-and-white.
3. Legal clearance for an anticipated-implementation statement and any Microsoft-supplied artwork.
4. Routing through Microsoft's Unicode standards owner without asking for a preferred Unicode review outcome.

The three organ proposals declined on 2022-11-04 remain useful future packet assets, but they should not be presented as eligible for the intake closing 2026-07-31. The Monday document should focus Microsoft's limited review time on the clearly re-eligible lead and challenger.
