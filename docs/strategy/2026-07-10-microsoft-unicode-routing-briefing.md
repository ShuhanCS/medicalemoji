# Purpose

This briefing identifies the people and internal routes most relevant to the health-related emoji L2 document and the accompanying individual emoji proposals.

## Recommended internal route

1. **David Rhew, MD - Microsoft medical sponsor**

   Owns the health rationale and coordinates the medical review.

2. **Heena Purohit - Microsoft co-author and coordinator**

   Coordinates the internal review package and routes it to the appropriate standards and product owners.

3. **Microsoft Unicode and standards representative**

   Confirms the correct UTC/ESR submission path, document-register process, and any internal Microsoft review requirements.

4. **Vishal Chowdhary - Microsoft Board member at Unicode**

   Provides the board-level internal decision point: whether Microsoft should sponsor and route the standards discussion. The Unicode Board page identifies Vishal Chowdhary as a Microsoft Vice President of Science and a 2026-present Board member.

5. **Peter Constable - UTC Chair; former Microsoft standards leader**

   Appropriate for process guidance and routing questions. He should not be asked to endorse a medical concept or promise an outcome. The UTC page identifies him as the current Chair.

6. **Microsoft Accessibility or Inclusive Design lead**

   Reviews small-size legibility, color and black-and-white accessibility, and platform-neutral visual paradigms for individual proposals.

7. **Microsoft Windows emoji, typography, or globalization lead**

   Reviews anticipated rendering and implementation considerations. This is technical input, not evidence of usage or a commitment to ship.

8. **Microsoft Legal/IP reviewer**

   Confirms artwork ownership, assignments, open-license provenance, and readiness for the Unicode Emoji Proposal Agreement and License.

## What each person should receive

**Rhew and Heena:** the L2, the individual proposal status matrix, and the decision brief.

**Vishal:** the one-page internal decision brief and the question: “Should Microsoft sponsor and route this package for internal standards review?”

**Microsoft standards representative:** the final L2 and the question: “What is the correct UTC/ESR route for a Microsoft-supported standards discussion?”

**Accessibility and Windows/typography reviewers:** only the individual proposal artwork and design notes relevant to their review.

**Legal/IP:** the individual proposal artwork provenance and licensing files.

## Message discipline

The L2 is a request for a standards review and report. It is not a request for Microsoft to guarantee encoding, adopt exact artwork, or treat sponsorship as usage evidence.

Each individual emoji proposal remains separate and must satisfy Unicode's published requirements for evidence, images, rights, and inclusion and exclusion analysis.

## Historical Microsoft Unicode contacts

The repository records Judy Safran-Aasen, Michele Coady, and Shelley Bjornstad as co-authors with Peter Constable on Microsoft's 2016 Unicode emoji data proposal, L2/16-228. Their current Microsoft roles are not confirmed, so they should be treated as historical references unless an internal contact confirms otherwise.

Cathy Wissink is also documented as a former Microsoft UTC leader and former UTC vice-chair. She is useful historical context, but should not be assumed to be a current Microsoft routing contact.
