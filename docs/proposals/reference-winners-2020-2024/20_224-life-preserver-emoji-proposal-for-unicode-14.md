2. Multiple usages
It is difficult to effectively and efficiently communicate relief, assistance, and
gratitude via text. Sometimes a small problem feels large. Sometimes a small
kindness makes a huge difference. A life preserver can visually communicate
the depth of a person's concern or gratitude, no matter the scope of the
problem or solution. A life preserver has a range of metaphorical uses in
addition to the literal application of being saved at sea.
