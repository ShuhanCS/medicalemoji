6. SELECTION FACTORS FOR EXCLUSION:
Overly specific:

     The proposed emoji is easily recognizable thanks to its black and white pattern. It is no
more specific than other animals that has been already included as emoji, such as 
BLOWFISH,  CRICKET or  SWAN.

Open ended:

     The orca is an specific species of animals, and not a set of symbol.

Already representable:

     The orca cannot be represented with currently existing emojis.

    The closest representation would be a black circle ( ), followed by a white circle ( )

and the whale emoji (), to indicate that is a whale with a black and white pattern.
     This is however pretty long, not scientifically correct (albeit popularly known as "killer

whales", they're a species of dolphins) and not easily understandable given the orcas are
considerably physically different to the rest of whales.

Logos, brands, UI icons, signage, specific people, specific
buildings, deities:

     The animal emoji does not represent any particular brand or part of any brand, nor any
UI icon, signage, person, building or deity.

Transient:

      Orcas are not a transient symbol, as they have been on planet Earth for about 11 million
of years.

     The only risk of orca becoming a unused symbol is in case of extinction, which is
possible due to sea pollution (according to the IUCN Red List), although unlikely.

Faulty comparison:

     The submission is due to its popularity and recognizeable pattern, and due to its own
merits according to its inclusion and exclusion patterns; and not due to inclusion of any other
symbol.

Exact images:

     The animal may be represented by different styles and images, and thus does not
require any exact image.
