Abstract

This proposal requests the addition of the "Trombone" Emoji to the Unicode library. A trombone is a
brass instrument played by moving a slide in and out to make different pitches. They are popular in pop
culture and places that enjoy jazz music. The trombone emoji could also be used to represent a mistake
symbolizing the "wop wop wop woooop" sounds when a person fails at a task.

C. Image Distinctiveness:

    The trombone is the only brass instrument with a slide (lower tube), which makes it instantly
    recognizable as a trombone. The trombone is longer and thinner than the other brass emojis and
    has its bell in a different location. The trombone does not have the distinctive circle of the postal
    horn, or the "cell" and "J" shapes of the trumpet and saxophone.

D. Completeness:

    As mentioned, a trombone would add another brass instrument that is widely recognized as being
    an orchestral instrument. The trombone has a slide that separates it from all other brass
    instruments. By adding a trombone to the emoji list, this would complement the other brass
    instruments, including "trumpet", and "saxophone".
