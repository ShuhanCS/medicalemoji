INTRODUCTION

Most organism eventually die including trees. A tree will die if it doesn't receive enough
water to survive. Water is main component of cells and main ingredient for
photosynthesis. Trees absorb water from their roots then transport it to the upper part
of the tree including the leaves using capillary system.
Trees signal their water deficit with various symptoms on its leaves, including change of
color to yellow or brown, leaf scorch, wilting leaves, and abscission (dropping their
leaves). Abscission is a common response against stress because it's advantageous for
the tree. Discarded leaves prevent further evaporation of water on the leaf surface and
provide more resources to other organ that are still alive. Abscission is common during
the dry season or during the autumn to prepare for the harsh winter.
Drought is a natural part of climate cycle, for million of years trees have adapted to
these harsh condition. But the climate is changing, drought become more frequent,
severe, and even spread to other places in short amount of time. Tree have various
adaption to survive these drought including discarding their leaves to conserve water,
but not for long. Eventually, all the resources that the tree have will be exhauted.
Insects and pathogen can attack a water deprived tree that unable to produce defensive
chemical. It results in death of portion or all parts of the tree.
