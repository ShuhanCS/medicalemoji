ABSTRACT

This proposal requests the addition of a TYRE emoji to the Unicode Emoji Standard. Tyres are a
distinctive part of every vehicle on the road and currently not recognised within the emojis. Tyres
can be found in every country on the world and are easily recognised by all users of emojis.

6) SELECTION FACTORS FOR EXCLUSION
F. OVERLY SPECIFIC

    A proposed TYRE emoji would not be overly specific, with over 1.42 billion cars on the road
    worldwide, the TYRE emoji would represent over 4 billion tyres globally
    (http://www.rfidtires.com/how-many-cars-world.html). There is no other more generic
    tyre/vehicle emoji that could be used to represent a tyre.

G. OPEN-ENDED
    A TYRE emoji would be the first of its specific type, as there is no current option for a tyre,
    and represents a distinctive product currently missing from the emoji keyboard.

H. ALREADY REPRESENTABLE
    There is currently no other emoji that could be used to represent a tyre.

I. LOGOS, BRANDS, UI ICONS, SIGNAGE, SPECIFIC PEOPLE, DEITIES
    The proposed TYRE emoji does not represent a specific logo, brand, UI icon, sign, person, or
    deity. There are no religious issues that would prohibit it from becoming a part of the emoji
    dictionary. No one person/ brand can hold a copy right, trademark or claim over the general
    shape of a tyre.

J. TRANSIENT
    The first vulcanized rubber pneumatic (inflatable) tyre was invented in 1845 and is unlikely
    to change its form. Tyres are a growing industry and with continued growth in interest
    online this would show a TYRE emoji to have consistent long-term usage.

K. FAULTY COMPARISON
    The is not currently an emoji for a tyre, there is no possibility for a fault comparison with any
    other emoji.

L. EXACT IMAGES
    The proposed TYRE emoji does not require an exact image to be distinguishable. It allows for
    many variations in colour and style that will still be identifiable given the basic form.
