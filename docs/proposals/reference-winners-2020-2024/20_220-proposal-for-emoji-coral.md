Breaking new ground
         Marine animals represent 9% of existing animal emojis. Corals are found all over the
         globe, covering about 1% of the Earth's surface, and coral houses over 25% of marine
         fish species. The inclusion of the coral would be a welcome addition to the marine animal
         category.

C. Image distinctiveness
Coral are very distinct due to their simple body shape and vibrant color.

D. Completeness
The inclusion of coral would more completely represent the variety of marine life represented by
emoji.

VI. Selection Factors -- Exclusion

E. Petitions of "frequent requests"
Coral and coral reefs emoji have been requested on Twitter, but to our knowledge no petition for
a CORAL emoji exists.

F. Overly-specific
The proposed CORAL emoji can be broadly used to represent many different types of coral, and
is no more specific than other animal emoji.

G. Open-ended
No, this emoji is not part of a similar set.

H. Already representable
There are no current animal emoji that can be used to represent CORAL.
I. Logos, brands, UI icons, signage, specific people, deities
There are several logos and brands that may display coral reef landscapes (e.g. NOAA Coral Reef
Conservation Program, Friends of Our Coral Reefs, Australian Coral Reef Society), but none are
similar to the presented emoji.

J. Transient
N/A. Coral reefs are major marine habitats that have persisted for hundreds of millions of years.
Because coral reefs are important culturally, economically, and environmentally, as well as being
common research animals/environments across the world, we expect a wide audience for the use
of a CORAL emoji.

K. Faulty comparison
N/A. Justification for encoding the proposed emoji does not depend on other emoji encoded only
for compatibility reasons.
