         C. Image distinctiveness

III. Selection factors -- Exclusion

         F. Overly specific
                   No. While there are many colors and types of wings this emoji is what has come to
                   become the prototypical style -- simple, white, and open..

         G. Open-ended
                   No. There are four types of Wing Shapes: Passive/Active Soaring, Elliptical wings,
                   High speed wings, and hovering wings. This wing is broad enough to reasonably
                   represent all four types.

         H. Already representable
                   No. While I originally thought perhaps the bird emoji was sufficiently emblematic
                   of "flying" , two major messaging platforms don't even show the bird emoji WITH
                   wings (really?? really.)

                   I also considered "airplane" emoji as a suitable "already representable" substitute
         but found that in identical sequences (listed above) it does not capture the

   long-standing range that a wing embodies
