4. Breaking new ground 

Fight cloud is undeniably iconic of a tumble, a clash, a disagreement. It is both

 visually and conceptually different from similar emoji such as collision , glove
  , and wrestlers . The fight cloud as a symbol dates back to comic strips

from the early 1900s, and unlike other existing emoji, specifically conveys

fighting that lasts across a period of time. As described in the book, The Visual

Language of Comics:

"Despite their similarities, interesting contrasts exist between fight clouds and
action stars. The events in a fight cloud must be durative: It represents a
continuous amount of time in which multiple actions take place. Try to imagine
the fight cloud in 3.1b as meaning just one punch: It does not work. In contrast,
action stars show punctive events, which are only a single action in time (like
one punch, kick, or explosion). Again, try to imagine the action star in 3.1a
covering multiple actions (say, punching twice or each boxer punching once),
and it will not work. In this way, single morphemes can convey different spans of
time and actions, especially if they are conventionalized into whole panels."

C. Image distinctiveness 
Could it be mistaken for "collision" emoji? Color and extra details help differentiate it. As
described in the book, The Visual Language of Comics:

"...fight clouds are far more specific in their meaning: They show a fight between
people. Because of this specificity, if these panels were to substitute for another, they
could only replace other panels showing a fight. Like action stars, however, these
panels do not explicitly show details of the fight (who is punching, kicking, biting,
pinching, etc.). Rather, it just conveys the general concept of fighting"

Lastly, "cartoony" and "superheroic" type comics appear all around the world at this point,
where fight clouds certainly appear. Other variants of inference clouds appear to show
transformations, love-making, or even cooking, so if the fight cloud emoji is depicted
general enough, it could be used for this purpose of "events that span across a duration of
time", beyond just a specific fight.

III. Selection factors -- Exclusion

         F. Overly specific
                   While there are a few different types of "fight clouds" (romantic, transformative,
                   fighting), this emoji was designed in a manner so it can be used to represent all
                   three -- especially when paired with others (see multiple uses).

         G. Open-ended
Auditing "visual morphology" that appears in manga (L2/19-390) there is
sufficient evidence to indicate that a "fight cloud" is not part of a set of similar
items which would result in more emoji proposals.

H. Already representable
 While there are emoji that express anger (
                                              ) none of these indicate a
  sustained fight. Moreover, the existing "fighting emoji" ( , ) are explicitly

physical and imply assault in a very real way. Fight cloud is clearly cartoonish,

silly, and non-threatening, and the fight itself is implied through inference not

through a direct representation of fighting.

While the collision emoji is the most conceptually similar emoji, this particular
emoji is useful in its extreme nature: It's explosive, it's loud, it's very real. Replace
"fight-cloud" emoji examples (Sections 2 & 3, above) with "collision" emoji and
the messages immediately become more violent, physical and explosive or
mean something else entirely:
