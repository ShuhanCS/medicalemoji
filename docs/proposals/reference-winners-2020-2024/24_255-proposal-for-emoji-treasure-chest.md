3. FACTORS FOR INCLUSION

     a. Multiple Meanings
          The concept of treasure is universal. It can apply to physical things with monetary value, but can also apply to
          emotional or sentimental items. While frequently associated with pirates, of a which an emoji already exists, a
          treasure chest has many other opportunities for use.
          � As a symbol for any valuable or collection of valuable items, especially in abundance. This value could
                either be monetary or sentimental.
          � Finding something that has been lost or hidden. The treasure chest is a widely understood symbol for
                something that has been uncovered. This is equally as applicable to discovering a new archaeological
                site as it is to finding an earring under a car seat or a keepsake in your grandma's attic.
          � A treasure trove is a term often used as a metaphor for a "valuable find." It is also used as a legal concept
                to determine ownership of discovered valuables.
          � Many video games incorporate "loot" or boxes of highly beneficial items.

     b. Use in Sequences

       The treasure chest can be combined with other emojis to indicate the search for something exciting but

      - A great lineup at a music festival
      - An extensive menu full of tasty food
      - An impressive selection at a car show
      - A cinematic classic/masterpiece
c. Breaks New Ground

Although there are already a few emojis that represent valuable items, that value is currently only monetary
  or physical. A sequence of coins
                           or a bag of money only represents wealth with no potential for
 alternative interpretations of emotional value. There are also multiple emojis that imply a search but none

that symbolize an item has been found or a discovery made. The treasure chest has a much more broad utility.

d. Distinctiveness
     The treasure chest as represented is a universal symbol. Even at a small resolution it is still distinguishable from
     a standard box or package. The open wooden chest with a rounded top and pile of gold coins is instantly
     recognizable.

4. FACTORS FOR EXCLUSION

     a. Already Representable
          No, there are no emojis that currently represent a general collection of valuables or the discovery of such
          an item. Any comparable sequence would have to consist of overly specific emojis that already exist at the
          exclusion of every other possible item not currently represented.

     b. Overly Specific
          While the proposed chest is filled with gold coins, as this is universally the quickest read of a treasure chest vs
          another kind of box, it can easily be interpreted as anything that holds value to the user.

     c. Open-ended
          No, this is not part of a set.

     d. Transient
          Treasure is a concept that has existed for thousands of years across many cultures. The symbol of the treasure
          chest as a representation of hidden or secret valuables has been a literary trope since the early 1800s and
          continues to be universally recognized today.

     e. Faulty Comparison
          Justification for encoding the proposed emoji does not depend on analogy with other emoji that were
          encoded only for compatibility reasons.

5. OTHER INFORMATION

     Considering the design, the contents of the treasure chest can be varied but for the sake of simplicity it is
     recommended that only gold coins be used. At small resolutions, other contents may become muddled and may not
     be as easily recognized.
