ABSTRACT

BIGFOOT is a mythological creature of many names across many parts of the world. From
the swamps of the Southeastern United States (known there as a Skunk Ape) to the peaks
of Asia where it's referenced as a Yeti, BIGFOOT continues to reverberate itself and it's
infamy in both our culture, media and tourism.

Many countries, states and cities tout being the "Home of Bigfoot", while countless
movies, shows and even concerts center around this elusive beast. There is even a
dedicated annual convention centered solely around Mr. Squatch. Claimed to have been
captured on camera countless times, it's most famous glimpse came from the
Patterson-Gimlin film, which depicts the large ape-like figure walking into the wood. This
film is where it's iconic stance and gait, as shown in the BIGFOOT emoji being proposed
comes from.

That same silhouette is now used throughout print and media, making it's presence on
stickers, advertisements and even used in memes on the internet and movies. But
BIGFOOT doesn't just represent the gentle giant himself. The creature's scarcity has
created a legend in its own right, that has only grown it's recognition - breeding a
multitude of hoaxes, gimmicks and cultural fascination. Fandom only continues to rise as
the internet breeds more fuel to the proverbial fire, and of the cryptozoological cast of
creatures, BIGFOOT continues to remain king.

         Breaking New Ground: The BIGFOOT emoji stands on it's own, representing a
         symbol of folklore that feels absent from the current Fantasy/Creature line-up.
         While the closest shape resemblance could be akin to PERSON WALKING or
         PERSON RUNNING, the BIGFOOT emoji embodies a culture and personality that

Distinctiveness: As mentioned above, there are currently no other emojis that accurately
and concisely represent the idea of BIGFOOT. Known for the infamous Patterson-Gimlin
photo, the gait and walking stance of BIGFOOT is immediately recognizable.
Completeness: N/A
Frequently Requested: BIGFOOT is not on the list as a frequently requested emoji, but is
often a reference in social media and other venues. It's absence amongst VAMPIRE,
ALIEN, FAIRY, etc. is apparent, and currently is only satisfied by using FOOT (which pales
in comparison).

SELECTION FACTORS - EXCLUSION

Overly Specific: BIGFOOT is not overly specific, as it represents both a mythological
creature as well as the culture and mystery it represents.
Open-Ended: I do not believe that the addition of TROLL will open up a new category of
emoji.
Already Representable: As stated earlier, there are no other emoji that currently
represent or come close to a combination that would easily represent BIGFOOT.

Logos, brands, other third-party IP rights, UI icons, signage, specific people, specific
buildings and landmarks, deities: BIGFOOT is none of the above. It is a generic
mythic/fantasy creature common in folklore.

Transient: BIGFOOT has only been increasing in popularity and culture. With more
countries and states leveraging this figure in tourism, as well as TV and movies
highlighting it, BIGFOOT is far from being a fad.

Faulty Comparison: N/A

Exact Images: This proposal does not request an exact image, but does identify that the
gait and stance of BIGFOOT should remain intact so as to remain recognizable by it's
infamous silhouette.

AUTHOR

Andrew Pignato (andrew@pignato.me) is a visual designer and artist with an avid passion
for UI/UX, emoji...and, of course, Bigfoot. He even once owned an old 1978 Jeep with a

and language of it all continues to shift, being able to capture this mythical beast and it's
surrounding culture into the annals of a global communication method would be a true
designer's "bucket list" item.
