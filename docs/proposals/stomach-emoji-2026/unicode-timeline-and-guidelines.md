## Current Unicode Format Requirements

The final PDF must include:

- Title: `Proposal for Emoji: Stomach`.
- Submitter names with one main point of contact.
- Date.
- Identification keywords and proposed emoji category.
- Color and black-and-white example images at 18x18 and 72x72 pixels at the top of the first page.
- Image rights/license statement.
- Factors for inclusion.
- Factors for exclusion.
- Required usage-frequency screenshots with `elephant` comparator where required.
- Publicly accessible PDF URL submitted through the official Unicode form.

## Required Frequency Evidence

Unicode requires screenshots from:

- Google Search.
- Google Video Search.
- Google Trends Web Search.
- Google Trends Image Search.
- Google Books Ngram Viewer.

Trends and Ngram screenshots must include `elephant` as a comparator.
