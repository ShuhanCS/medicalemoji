# Kidney Emoji Decline Date And Submission Update

Last checked: 2026-05-13

## Practical Finding

The exact public decline date for the 2022 `KIDNEYS` proposal is not exposed in Unicode's public proposal status sheet. The public sheet confirms only:

| Emoji | Public status | Date submitted |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kidney | Declined | 2019-12-17 |
| KIDNEYS | Declined | 2022-07-19 |

The strongest public timing evidence is the Emoji Subcommittee report for UTC #173. That report is dated 2022-10-31 and says the ESC had completed review of 2022 proposals and that authors would be notified shortly after the 2022 Q4 UTC meeting. UTC #173 met 2022-11-01 through 2022-11-03.

Practical interpretation: the `KIDNEYS` decline likely happened or was communicated around early November 2022, not on the 2022-07-19 submission date. If the four-year bar is counted from decline or notification, the proposal may not be eligible before the current 2026 intake deadline of 2026-07-31. This should be verified directly with Unicode/ESR or from the original rejection email before filing.

## Current 2026 Template Requirements

The Unicode emoji proposal guidelines page says:

- Last update: 2026-04-02.
- Current intake window: accepting submissions until 2026-07-31.
- The body text still contains stale 2025 date language, so any date-sensitive filing should be checked against Unicode/ESR.

Current required proposal structure:

1. Title: `Proposal for Emoji: <name>`.
2. Submitter names, separated by semicolons when there are multiple submitters.
3. One main point of contact.
4. Date, updated on revised proposals.
5. Identification section with keywords and proposed category.
6. Images section with color and black-and-white examples at 18x18 and 72x72 pixels.
7. Image rights/license statement.
8. Factors for inclusion.
9. Factors for exclusion.
10. Other useful information.

Current evidence requirements:

- Google Search screenshot with result count.
- Google Video Search screenshot with result count.
- Google Trends Web Search screenshot.
- Google Trends Image Search screenshot.
- Google Books Ngram Viewer screenshot.
- Trends and Ngram evidence must include `elephant` as the comparison term.
- Use a private/incognito browser where possible.
- Use the widest available date range.
- Include supplemental non-English evidence when appropriate.

Evidence that does not count:

- Petitions.
- Hashtags.
- Social media posts asking for the emoji.
- Anecdotes.
- Society support letters as frequency evidence.

Proposal risks for kidney:

- Unicode explicitly says declined emoji are not eligible for re-review within four years.
- The 2022 `KIDNEYS` decline-date clock is unresolved.
- The kidney proposal must not rely primarily on disease-awareness or cause framing.
- The proposal must avoid a faulty comparison argument such as "heart and lungs exist, so kidney should exist."
- The visual design must be recognizable at 18x18 and distinct from beans.
- Image rights must be clean enough for Unicode's broad license.
