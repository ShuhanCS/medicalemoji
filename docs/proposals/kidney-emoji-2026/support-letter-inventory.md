## Proposal Use

Use these letters to show:

- The renewed proposal has a real nephrology coalition behind it.
- The candidate is not a one-person request.
- Kidney communication matters to patient organizations, clinicians, international nephrology groups, transplant/dialysis communities, and public-health educators.

Do not use these letters as substitutes for:

- Google Search frequency evidence.
- Google Video Search frequency evidence.
- Google Trends Web Search screenshots.
- Google Trends Image Search screenshots.
- Google Books Ngram screenshots.
- Image-rights documentation.
- Re-review eligibility confirmation.
