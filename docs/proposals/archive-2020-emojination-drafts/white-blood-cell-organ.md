# Abstract

We are asking for the addition of a WHITE BLOOD CELL (OBJECT) emoji.
White blood cells, or leukocytes, are key cells in the body’s immune
system used to fight off infections, parasites, disease, among other
potential maladies. White blood cells are also associated with serious
diseases in humans, such as HIV/AIDS, leukemia, and sepsis. As such, the
emojification of the white blood cell would aid medical professionals
and educators to teach the broader public on the significance and
importance of the white blood cell.

# Introduction

Discovered in 1843, white blood cells are grown in bone marrow and
deployed in the body to fight infections and heal wounds. There are
several categories of white blood cells granulocytes, which include
neutrophils (the most common white blood cell), basophils, and
eosinophils. Other types of white blood cells include lymphocytes
(commonly known as T-cells and B-cells) and monocytes. Each type serves
a different purpose and protects the human body from different types of
infections, and while each appears slightly differently, the standard
representation of white blood cells applies to them all.

*[figure: image1.jpg]*

White blood cells also feature in some of the most pressing diseases
facing humanity. HIV, which infected 1.9 million people worldwide in
2019, targets and infects certain white blood cells and weakens the
immune system. Lymphoma, which infects around 80,000 people per year in
the US alone, targets lymphocytes. Leukemia develops from bone marrow,
the area of production for white blood cells, and affects around 60,000
people in the US per year. Myeloma, also a blood cancer targeting white
blood cells, infects 30,000 Americans per year.

*[figure: image2.jpg]*

The medical importance of white blood cells, both to document their
purpose and educate on the diseases that target them warrant the cell
being visualized in the emoji format. As tele-helath trends continue,
adding symbols like this for medical professionals to convey their
diagnoses would ease the healthcare transition to a more digital
landscape. Additionally, the importance of the white blood cell to the
human body would be a useful emoji for a myriad of professionals to
depict this important cell on a digital level.

WHITE BLOOD CELLS are circular, like many existing emojis, but have a
distinct outer layer of membrane blebs to differentiate it from existing
circular emojis.

## Completeness

There is no emoji to represent the immune system. Adding a white blood
cell emoji would represent all types of white blood cells and introduce
a symbol to portray the immune system. Existing medical emojis represent
the respiratory (LUNGS), vascular (HEART), and other systems, but the
primary weapon the body uses to defend these systems - the WHITE BLOOD
CELL - is not represented.

# Selection Factors – Exclusion

## Petitions or Frequently Requested

Not Applicable.

## Overly Specific

The WHITE BLOOD CELL is a cell every human produces hundreds of billions
of times each day. It serves and represents a function of our immune
system central to our health. As such it has general use and is
applicable both to the broad public and the medical community.

## Open-ended

There are few cells in the human body as important as white blood cells.
As the medical emoji category is limited, some expansion is to be
expected, but few cells and organs are so important and widely known as
the white blood cell to cause an infinite number of new medical-related
emojis.

## Already Representable

No existing emoji represents the immune system. The DROP OF BLOOD
(MEDICAL) contains white blood cells, but does not represent the immune
system as it is quite general. For medical professionals and educators
looking to teach children about their immune system, the white blood
cell emoji represents a clear and distinct articulation of the immune
system.

## Logos, brands, UI icons, signage, specific people, specific landmarks, deities 

There are no logos, brands, deities, specific people or landmarks

## Transient

WHITE BLOOD CELLS (MEDICAL) are a cell in the human immune system, and
therefore can expect consistent use and applicability.

## Faulty Comparison

We are mostly comparing the WHITE BLOOD CELL to the DROP OF BLOOD emoji,
but very rarely and believe that since white blood cells are a component
in blood, it is a fair comparison. Otherwise, the white blood cell emoji
is relatively distinct from the existing set of emojis and thus is not
being argued for in comparison to other emojis.

## Exact Images

WHITE BLOOD CELLS (MEDICAL) have some distinct features, such as the
membrane blebs that appear on the cell’s membrane. Otherwise, the cells
vary by category and allow for some variation in representation.
