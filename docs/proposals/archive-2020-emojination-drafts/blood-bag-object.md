# **Abstract**

This proposal requests the addition of a BLOOD BAG (OBJECT) emoji. A
blood bag is exactly what it sounds like, a bag that holds blood, often
with added chemicals to preserve and prevent clotting of the blood.
Prepackaged blood plays an important role in healthcare and the blood
bag itself represents a larger meaning in blood transfusions, blood
donation, and blood products (like red blood cells, platelets, clotting
factors).

People around the world require blood transfusions for many different
reasons from genetic anemias to profound blood loss. Regardless of the
indication, medically administered blood has saved thousands of lives
over the last hundred years. About every 2 seconds, someone in the world
needs blood products. Some people need blood once or twice in their
life, simply because they have lost too much, while others need blood
throughout their life. The ability to communicate about this process
without needing any technical details or training would be incredibly
helpful for patients. This highlights an important opportunity to
improve public health by making available a BLOOD BAG emoji for digital
communication.

# **Introduction**

Human-to-human blood transfusions started to enter the field of
scientific research and medical practice in the beginning of the 20th
century when doctors and researchers started to learn about blood types.
As scientists learned more about what makes up a blood cell,
transfusions grew to be safer and more effective. In the United States
alone nearly 21 million blood products are administered every year,
which is more than 55,000 per day. Estimates for global use exceed 100
million blood products administered every year.

For some patients, receiving blood is a life saving measure because they
have had severe bleeding. For other patients, like those with Sickle
Cell disease or Thalassemia intermedia, physical functionality is
dependent on receiving blood routinely. On the other hand, people with
hemochromatosis require blood draws throughout their life in order to
prevent toxic levels of iron accumulating in their organs. For some
people giving or receiving blood is simply a part of their life.

*[figure: image1.png]*

In addition to representing the giving or receiving of blood, the BLOOD
BAG emoji can also represent treatments related to the blood like
antibiotics or chemotherapy and blood-related diseases like anemia (low
amount of red blood cells) or leukemia (cancer of the blood). Anemia
alone is present in about 25% of the world population. The BLOOD BAG can
represent blood levels and could help someone easily understand what
their lab results mean. With emoji representing hemoglobin/hematocrit,
there would be less confusion and easier communication.

As the need for blood grows with a population, more attention has to be
directed to availability of blood types. Blood type is a universal
system to describe the presence or absence of certain proteins on the
surface or red blood cells that can trigger a strong immune reaction. If
the red blood cells made within the person’s body are made with these
proteins, then they will not have that severe reaction. This is why
someone who is AB+ can receive all types of blood, because they
naturally make red blood cells with all three proteins (A, B, and Rh).
This is also why O- blood can be given to anyone, because it does not
have any of the three surface proteins. Because blood type O negative is
compatible with anyone, it is often overused and in short supply. The
use of this blood should be restricted to persons with O negative blood,
as nothing else is compatible with them, and women who might be pregnant
and for whom it would be impossible to do blood group testing before
giving them emergency treatment. A BLOOD BAG emoji would be an effective
tool to help doctors, public health officials, and scientists
communicate and educate about blood types and help address the frequent
shortage of O- blood.

We believe with how frequently blood products and blood bags are used
and how many people need blood, the BLOOD BAG emoji would be used
frequently and would serve to improve public health by easing a person’s
communication of complicated conditions, treatments, or procedures.

- Receiving a blood donation

## Image Distinctiveness

The BLOOD BAG would look like a generic representation of the bags used
worldwide for storing and administering blood. It is similar in
appearance to an IV bag, but is distinct in both color (red instead of
clear) and labeling (blood type).

## Completeness

The BLOOD BAG helps bring completeness to the medical object category
with STETHOSCOPE, SYRINGE, MICROSCOPE, DROP OF BLOOD, PETRI DISH, TEST
TUBE, and THERMOMETER.

# Selection Factors – Exclusion

## Petitions or Frequently Requested

Not Applicable.

## Overly Specific

CT SCAN is a prevalent medical imaging technique with estimates of more
than 70 million scans in 2015 in the US alone. The CT SCAN can also
represent medical imaging as a whole and could signify X-Ray, MRI,
Ultrasound, and others.

## Open-ended

This is not open ended, as CT SCAN would be able to represent many forms
of medical imaging. With a CT SCAN emoji, MRI SCAN would not be
necessary.

## Already Representable

While there are a number of emojis representing medical diagnostic
equipment like STETHOSCOPE, SYRINGE, and MICROSCOPE there is not an
emoji for any radiologic diagnostic processes. Just as most people have
had a doctor listen to their heart, had blood drawn, or had special
tests, most people have had some form of radiologic diagnostic procedure
like a CT Scan, X-Ray, or MRI

## Logos, brands, UI icons, signage, specific people, specific landmarks, deities 

There are no logos, brands, deities, specific people or landmarks
represented by this.

## Transient

Medical technology is advancing very quickly, but as long as people need
blood, blood replacement products will be needed. Because blood type is
so important for people who need blood, the standard format of blood bag
labeling will continue so blood type errors are minimized. As a result,
blood bags will be around for a long time and will continue to have the
standard labeling for blood typing.

## Faulty Comparison

There are no comparisons made outside of the comparison to DROP OF BLOOD
which shows the relative decrease in searches for images with both DROP
OF BLOOD and BLOOD BAG.

exact imitation or extreme detail for the concept to be communicated.
