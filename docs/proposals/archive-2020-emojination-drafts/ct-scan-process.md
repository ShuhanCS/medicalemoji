# Abstract

This proposal requests the addition of a CT SCAN emoji. The “CT” stands
for computed tomography which means the scanner uses computers and
algorithms (computed) to create cross-sectional images of the person
scanned (tomography). The CT SCAN involves rotating X-rays and powerful
computing to yield a detailed image with three dimensions of
information. The CT SCAN plays an important role in the diagnostic
process which gives medical professionals a noninvasive, relatively
quick, and very accurate way to assist in diagnosing many diseases from
strokes to infections.

Because of its clinical effectiveness, the use of the CT SCAN continues
to grow worldwide. Studies have estimated around a 10% increase in the
number of scans per patient in several countries and some countries are
already averaging around 300 scans per 1000 people. This increased
utilization has raised concern for some doctors, scientists, and
researchers as the radiation exposure has been linked to multiple
cancers especially when the radiation exposure is during childhood.
There is ongoing discussion about the balance between the risk of
radiation and the usefulness of quick and accurate diagnostic data. This
highlights an important opportunity to improve public health by making
available a CT SCAN emoji for digital communication.

# Introduction

The CT Scan first entered medical practice in 1970 and has improved
medicine drastically ever since. Because the scan is quicker and cheaper
than an MRI and is much more detailed and precise than an X-ray, it has
saved countless lives in its 50 years of utilization by timely detection
of strokes, clots, infections, and many other diseases. Another tool
that physicians will sometimes use is contrast dye which the patient
either drinks or has it injected through their veins. Contrast can help
better visualize organs and blood vessels.

The CT represents a foundational diagnostic procedure and millions of
people have them done every year. Despite the widespread use of
diagnostic imaging like the CT Scan, X-ray, Ultrasound, and MRI, this
would be the first emoji to represent that category aside from the
RADIATION emoji. In fact, a google image search of X-ray Emoji yields
multiple images and clipart that can be copy-pasted as well as an image
of CROSS MARK and FLASHLIGHT which is meant to communicate X-ray.

*[figure: image6.png]*

When a patient is being subjected to a CT scan there is often a
significant amount of anxiety involved. The anxiety is often a
combination of their lack of knowledge surrounding medical imaging, the
potential disease that warranted a CT Scan, as well as the confined
space of a CT Scanner. This anxiety happens frequently enough that some
physicians will prescribe a medication for anxiety whenever they send
patients for a CT. Familiarity with the process of scanning, the
appearance of the scanner, and what the scanner is doing would serve to
relieve some of the anxiety related to receiving a CT Scan. The addition
of the CT SCAN (ORGAN) emoji would promote clearer, more accessible
communication between healthcare professionals and patients and would
increase general knowledge and comfort with diagnostic imaging. Reducing
barriers to open discussions of the risk factors and rationale for
imaging has the potential to raise global awareness and thereby decrease
anxiety surrounding CT Scans.

### Multiple Usages

The CT SCAN emoji can also be used to represent the following:

- Medical imaging in a more generic meaning (especially MRI imaging
  because there is a similar appearance between the MRI scanner and the
  CT scanner)

- Can represent scientific experiments and objective studies similar to
  MICROSCOPE or TEST TUBE emojis.

- A section of a hospital or building related to radiology.

## Image Distinctiveness

The CT SCAN is similar in appearance to an MRI Scan, but because both
represent detailed medical imaging, the distinction would not be
important. Otherwise, the CT SCAN looks unlike any other object,
process, or other emoji.

## Completeness

The CT SCAN helps bring completeness to the medical object category with
STETHOSCOPE, ALEMBIC, SYRINGE, MICROSCOPE, DROP OF BLOOD, PETRI DISH,
TEST TUBE, and THERMOMETER.

# Selection Factors – Exclusion

## Petitions or Frequently Requested

Not Applicable.

## Overly Specific

CT SCAN is a prevalent medical imaging technique with estimates of more
than 70 million scans in 2015 in the US alone. The CT SCAN can also
represent medical imaging as a whole and could signify X-Ray, MRI,
Ultrasound, and others.

## Open-ended

This is not open ended, as CT SCAN would be able to represent many forms
of medical imaging. With a CT SCAN emoji, MRI SCAN would not be
necessary.

## Already Representable

While there are a number of emojis representing medical diagnostic
equipment like STETHOSCOPE, SYRINGE, and MICROSCOPE there is not an
emoji for any radiologic diagnostic processes. Just as most people have
had a doctor listen to their heart, had blood drawn, or had special
tests, most people have had some form of radiologic diagnostic procedure
like a CT Scan, X-Ray, or MRI

## Logos, brands, UI icons, signage, specific people, specific landmarks, deities 

There are no logos, brands, deities, specific people or landmarks
represented by this.

## Transient

Medicinal technology is advancing very quickly and diagnostic workup
continues to grow and change. Nonetheless, the CT SCAN innately
represents medical imaging and could continue to signify medical imaging
even if CT SCANS are reduced in the future.

## Faulty Comparison

The only comparisons made with the CT SCAN are other medical diagnostic
equipment to emphasize that CT SCAN would help complete the medical
objects category, but CT SCAN plays a unique role in meaning without any
comparison.

## Exact Images

The basic image of a CT SCAN involves a large ring-like structure and a
table, which the patient lays on, that runs through the ring. This image
does not necessitate any exact imitation or extreme detail for the
concept to be communicated.
