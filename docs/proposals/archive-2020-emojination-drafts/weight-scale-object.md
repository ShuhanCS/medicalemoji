# Abstract

We are requesting the addition of a WEIGHT SCALE (OBJECT) emoji. The
WEIGHT SCALE (OBJECT) is a common consumer and medical device used to
measure a person’s weight and can also display similar measurements like
BMI or muscle mass. Weight scales can be useful tools to track health
improvements and have been shown to be effective in causing weight loss.
That being said, there can also be unhealthy side effects with frequent
weight observation, such as associating a certain weight with a healthy
lifestyle.

We believe the addition of a weight scale emoji will help combat
negative side effects of constant weight scale usage and reinforce their
positive benefits. From using an emoji as a periodic reminder to weigh
oneself to granting professionals the digital capability to convey
healthy lifestyle choices, we believe the WEIGHT SCALE emoji will be
popular and help people create a healthier relationship between their
weight goals and their lifestyle.

# Introduction

The first patent for the digital weight scale was applied for in 1980 by
<span class="mark">Richard Loshbough and Edward Pryor. Since then,
digital scales have become more sophisticated and able to measure more
values of interest to users and medical professionals. Although numbers
are hard to come by, much of the public either have weight scales at
home or have easy access to them and can use them to track their own
weight and other health indicators. This presents health professionals
with a common symbol which, if used properly, could be a helpful tool to
a healthier public.</span>

<span class="mark">The domestication of weight management can be a
useful tool in fighting worldwide obesity epidemics. The simplest
measurement, a person’s weight, can be a valuable indicator in tracking
a person’s weight loss journey to live a healthier lifestyle. Beyond
weight, weight scales are now equipped to measure more complex
measurements, such as body fat percentage, that give medical
professionals a more holistic view on someone’s health. In the United
States, the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention estimated 42.4%
of American adults were obsese in 2018, an increase of around 12% since
the 1990s. Obesity is associated with higher risk for heart disease,
diabetes, and other preventable, but high risk diseases. Economically,
obesity cripples a country’s healthcare system, as obese people are
require significantly more medical care than patients of normal weight.
The seriousness of obesity necessitates various tools to educate,
monitor, and combat a person’s health indicators.</span>

<span class="mark">While obesity epidemics are serious medical
emergencies, the fixation on a certain weight to indicate health is
another serious medical problem. Anorexia nervosa is a common
physiological condition in which people, overwhelming teenage aged
girls, fixate on weight loss to an unhealthy point. The ease of access
to weight scales may contribute to this disorder and cause stress at the
slightest of weight gains, especially given the way weight fluctuates
during the day. Persistent measurements can have negative repercussions
for the user.</span>

<span class="mark">This highlights the importance of medical dialogue on
the proper way to approach weight. Used effectively, weight tracking can
help combat obesity, while maintaining a healthy relationship with one’s
body. Granting medical professionals a symbol to remind patients to
record their weight and other health indicators displayed by weight
scales can help produce tailored plans to lose or gain weight to a
healthy level. This would help fight two serious health problems,
obesity and anorexia, at once. Thus, we believe the addition of a WEIGHT
SCALE (OBJECT) emoji would improve public health.</span>

### **Multiple Usages**

WEIGHT SCALES (OBJECT) are associated with weight tracking for health
purposes. Whether someone is looking to gain or lose weight, people will
use weight scales to track progress towards a weight goal. The weight
scale emoji could become a shorthand for one’s weight, whether as a
question or a statement.

The emoji resembles the food scales used by home cooks and restaurants
to measure produce, spices, and other ingredients.

### **Use in sequences** 

Some possible sequences would be:

- WEIGHT SCALE + Activity emoji → WORKING OUT TO LOSE WEIGHT

### **Breaking new ground**

Somewhat. The BALANCE SCALE (OBJECT) broke ground by representing a
scale, but it more so represents the balance of law and is recognizable
as a tool used in food markets. The primary purpose of a weight scale,
to weigh humans and other animals, cannot be accomplished by a balance
scale.

## Image Distinctiveness

The WEIGHT SCALE (OBJECT) somewhat resembles other square-shaped emojis,
such as the FLOPPY DISK (OBJECT) or CLIPBOARD (OBJECT). What
distinguishes the weight scale emoji from other square emojis is the
clear digital display at the top of the symbol that would show a weight.
Even at an emoji scale, the digital display is clear enough to represent
a weight scale.

## Completeness

The only existing scale emoji, the BALANCE SCALE (OBJECT), does a good
job representing certain situations in which that type of scale is most
applicable, including more philosophical notions of the balance of the
law. The weight scale, however, fills a gap for weighing humans,
animals, and other products more commonly weighed with a digital scale.

# Selection Factors – Exclusion

## Petitions or Frequently Requested

Not Applicable.

## Overly Specific

WEIGHT SCALES (OBJECT) are applicable in many scenarios, whether in the
gym, a home, or a hospital. Many people have weight scales and therefore
it is not a niche emoji, but rather one with general applicability.

## Open-ended

The WEIGHT SCALE (OBJECT) is a very common tool used by medical
professionals and by the broader public. It exists in doctor’s offices,
bathrooms, and gyms. It is a relatively cheap tool that has broad
recognition and use. Therefore it is not open-ended, but a necessary
addition that will close gaps in the emoji selection rather than open
them more.

## Already Representable

There is a dearth of current emojis that represent the medical purpose
of a WEIGHT SCALE (OBJECT). The BALANCE SCALE (OBJECT) does partially
align with the weight scale, but is more so used to depict the balance
of the law and justice and, in some cases, is used in food markets to
weigh goods. The purpose of the WEIGHT SCALE, to weigh humans, is not
represented in the current emoji selection.

## Logos, brands, UI icons, signage, specific people, specific landmarks, deities 

There are no logos, brands, deities, specific people or landmarks
represented by this. Many companies create weight scales, but this emoji
makes no particular reference to a certain brand.

## Transient

The prototypical weight scale has evolved as technology advancements
have allowed for a slimmer model. The next 50 years may bring slight
changes to the current typical model, but it is doubtful any
advancements would radically change the model to the point this emoji
will be unrecognizable in 50 years as a weight scale.

## Faulty Comparison

We are not comparing WEIGHT SCALE (OBJECT) to any existing emoji, other
than BALANCE SCALES (OBJECT). Whereas balance scales connote justice and
courts or food markets, weight scales are used for weighing a person. We
believe there is a gap in the medical emoji options that a weight scale
would fill, but are not arguing against the balance scale emoji.

## Exact Images

There can be some variability in representation while still portraying a
WEIGHT SCALE (OBJECT). Many different companies produce weight scales,
so there already exists variation in representation while still
recognizable as a weight scale.
