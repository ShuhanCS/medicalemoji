## Why this matters

Unicode's guidelines state: "Emoji declined within the last four years are not eligible for
re-review." The Emoji Proposals Status spreadsheet records two Kidney declines:

| Emoji | Status | Date Submitted |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kidney | Declined | 2019-12-17 |
| KIDNEYS | Declined | 2022-07-19 |

The project also holds a status notice dated **2022-11-04**.

- Four years from the spreadsheet's submitted date (2022-07-19) lands **2026-07-19** — twelve days
  before the 2026-07-31 deadline, so eligible.
- Four years from the decision/notice date (2022-11-04) lands **2026-11-04** — after the deadline,
  so not eligible.

The Submission FAQ phrases it as "emoji whose proposals **were declined** within the last four
years," which points toward the decision date. This is unresolved and it gates everything else.
Filing while ineligible risks a procedural decline that could restart the clock.
