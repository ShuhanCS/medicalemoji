# Who can help with the Medical Emoji review

This is the accessible text companion to
`output/pdf/2026-07-13-who-can-help-with-medical-emoji-review.pdf`.

## Route 1: individual emoji proposals

1. Shuhan He completes and files each selected proposal.
2. The proposal enters through Unicode's official Emoji Submission Form, the required route for 2026
   proposal review.
3. The Emoji Standard & Research Working Group reviews the submission and makes recommendations.
4. The Unicode Technical Committee retains authority over technical decisions.

Every candidate follows this route separately.

## Route 2: health-coverage discussion document

1. Shuhan He and Microsoft's standards team may revise or coauthor the draft, or Microsoft may use it as
   source material for its own document.
2. The document follows Unicode's separate UTC document-submission process and requests an agenda
   disposition.
3. The UTC may discuss the question or refer work.
4. ESR may advise whether additional guidance or review would help.

The discussion document cannot replace an individual emoji proposal.

## Microsoft roles David Rhew can connect

- **Microsoft Unicode standards lead or current UTC delegate:** checks the process and timing, reviews the
  discussion draft, and may request normal agenda consideration.
- **Emoji, font, and accessibility reviewers:** test the 18-pixel color and black-and-white artwork and
  platform-neutral implementation feasibility.
- **Microsoft legal or IP:** needed only if Microsoft contributes artwork, coauthors a document, or approves
  an implementation-support statement.
- **Optional executive routing:** Vishal Chowdhary is Microsoft's Unicode Board director. The Board does not
  decide which emoji are encoded.
