## 6. Background one-pager (attach or paste if she wants detail)

- **Precedent:** Anatomical heart 🫀 and lungs 🫁 approved in Emoji 13.0 (2020) after a
  medical-emoji proposal led by Shuhan He, MD; implemented by Apple in iOS 14.2. Covered by
  the *Boston Globe* and *The Verge*.
- **Peer-reviewed basis:** Lai D, Lee J, He S. "Emoji for the Medical Community —
  Challenges and Opportunities." *JAMA.* 2021;326(9):795-796. And: He S, et al. "Comparison
  of an Emoji-Based Visual Analog Scale With a Numeric Rating Scale for Pain Assessment."
  *JAMA.* 2022;328(2):208-209.
- **Why it matters clinically:** plain-language patient education, telehealth,
  cross-language communication, and public-health messaging — interoperable, standardized
  symbols that work across every phone, portal, and platform.
- **Coalition to date:** physician-society and patient-organization letters across the six
  proposed concepts; ACEP as the first national multi-specialty society endorser.
- **The ask in one line:** a national-academy-level voice of support, plus any personal
  door to Unicode, in time for World Emoji Day (July 17) and our August 1 deadline.
