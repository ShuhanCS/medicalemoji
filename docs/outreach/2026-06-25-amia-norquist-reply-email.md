Hi Craig,

Thank you — this is exactly the kind of help I was hoping for, and I really appreciate you
turning it around so fast. Bringing it to the AMIA board for review is the perfect path, and the
"other professional society backing" point is right on: ACEP signed on as the first national
multi-specialty endorser, and we now hold letters from the AGA, ASGE, the American Society of
Nephrology, the International Society of Nephrology, the National Kidney Foundation, KDIGO, and
roughly a dozen more.

**Dr. Lingam — wonderful to meet you, and thank you for being willing to take a look.** Craig
mentioned you chair the Clinical Informatics Program Directors group within AMIA, which makes
this feel even more on-mission: medical emoji are, at bottom, standardized clinical iconography —
a controlled-vocabulary, interoperability, and patient-communication problem, which is squarely
AMIA's world. I'd be glad to brief you and your colleagues at whatever level of detail is useful.

I've attached two things to make this turnkey for the board:

1. **A one-pager** — the precedent (we helped get the anatomical heart 🫀 and lungs 🫁 into the
   Unicode Standard in 2020), the evidence base (JAMA 2021/2022, JAMA Network Open 2023), the
   2026 concepts (kidney, liver, stomach, spine, white blood cell, and EKG), the coalition so
   far, and exactly what we're asking AMIA for.
2. **A draft letter of support** — written in AMIA's voice and modeled on the ACEP letter, ready
   for the board to edit freely, place on AMIA letterhead, and sign. It commits AMIA to nothing
   beyond the letter itself; we handle the Unicode submissions, the usage evidence, and the
   artwork.

The ask in one line: an AMIA-level letter affirming the informatics and health-literacy value of
a standardized set of medical emoji and encouraging Unicode to consider them — nothing more.

On timing, there's a natural target. **World Emoji Day is July 17** (the usual moment to publish
letters of support), and our working deadline to have everything in hand is **August 1**
(Unicode's intake closes July 31). Anything the board is comfortable lending by **mid-July**
would be ideal — but there's genuinely no pressure, and we'll move at whatever pace and process
AMIA prefers. Happy to present to the board or the right committee, in person or by video, if
that's useful.

Thank you both — this means a lot.

Best,
Shuhan
