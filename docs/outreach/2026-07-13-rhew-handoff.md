## Suggested reading order

1. **Health-related emoji coverage** is the final three-page UTC-format paper. It is separate from the
   individual emoji proposals and asks UTC to consider referring a narrow set of questions to the Emoji
   Standard & Research Working Group.

   https://github.com/ShuhanCS/medicalemoji/blob/codex/eligible-2026-slate/output/pdf/2026-07-13-health-related-emoji-coverage-l2-submission.pdf

2. **Medical Emoji submission options** is the 90-page bookmarked packet. It begins with the decision brief
   and includes all 15 working proposals. CT Scan and Blood Bag are planned after revision; Pill Box is the
   first alternate. The other files retain their evidence, review, draft, or later-cycle labels.

   https://github.com/ShuhanCS/medicalemoji/blob/codex/eligible-2026-slate/output/pdf/2026-07-13-medical-emoji-submission-options-packet.pdf

3. **Who can help route the Medical Emoji work** is the three-page Microsoft and Unicode contact map. It
   identifies the strongest first contacts, the two official submission routes, the current public leadership,
   and the Microsoft roles that still need to be confirmed.

   https://github.com/ShuhanCS/medicalemoji/blob/codex/eligible-2026-slate/output/pdf/2026-07-13-who-can-help-with-medical-emoji-review.pdf

4. **Individual proposal PDFs** is a ZIP containing exactly the 15 proposal PDFs. The filenames show the
   current readiness category. The UTC paper and the 90-page packet are kept outside the ZIP because they are
   not individual emoji submissions.

   https://github.com/ShuhanCS/medicalemoji/raw/refs/heads/codex/eligible-2026-slate/output/zip/2026-07-13-medical-emoji-potential-submissions-pdfs.zip
