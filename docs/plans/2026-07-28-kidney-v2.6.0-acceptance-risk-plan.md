# Kidney v2.6.0 Acceptance-Risk Revision Plan

## Goal

Create a stronger review packet without claiming evidence that the team does not have. The revision addresses
three acceptance risks in the Unicode-facing proposal and records a separate, evidence-gated route to a positive
Compatibility factor.

## Audience

The proposal is written for Unicode emoji reviewers applying the live 2026 selection factors. The compatibility
spec is written for the Microsoft and Medical Emoji collaborators who can obtain product evidence.

## Decisions

1. **Compatibility:** Keep the formal factor `Not applicable` until the evidence gates in
   `docs/specs/2026-07-28-kidney-compatibility-evidence-spec.md` are met. A Windows or Teams search screenshot
   alone is useful product evidence but does not prove high-frequency use of an existing non-Unicode kidney
   emoji. Do not relabel the CLDR annotation mismatch as formal Compatibility.
2. **Frequency scope:** Retain the required unfiltered `kidney` searches. Remove the self-defeating disclaimer
   that argues against Unicode's required proxy. State positively and accurately that the five required sources
   measure sustained use of the proposed name, then connect that evidence to cited kidney-specific message
   contexts. Do not substitute `kidney organ`, which would undercount the proposed concept and depart from the
   required name-based protocol.
3. **Evidence freshness:** Attempt to refresh all five screenshots in a private Firefox session. Preserve the
   exact query, settings, visible result count where required, and capture date. Retain the existing valid,
   dated exhibits rather than replace them with a count-free, CAPTCHA, unusual-traffic, or rate-limit page.
4. **Open-endedness:** Replace the neighbor-list defense with a reproducible threshold test. Kidney is supported
   by the combination of required frequency evidence, failed existing representation, several established
   message sequences, and a distinctive 18x18 pictograph. State that another organ would need to establish its
   own case against every selection factor; encoding Kidney creates no automatic anatomy series.
