# Stomach v2.4.0 Interoperability Plan

Date: 2026-07-28

## Audience and objective

The public proposal is for Unicode Emoji Standard & Research reviewers evaluating a 2026 Stomach submission.
Issue a new v2.4.0 packet that follows the current Unicode proposal order, makes the existing-user benefit and
empirical evidence easy to find, and treats compatibility and interoperability honestly.

- Unicode proposal guidelines last updated 2026-05-20:
  `https://unicode.org/emoji/proposals.html`
- ESR priorities for Emoji 18.0 and beyond, including Emoji 19.0 intake:
  `https://www.unicode.org/L2/L2025/25128-esr-priorities.pdf`

  `docs/proposals/TEMPLATE-emoji-proposal.md`

## Work

1. Preserve v2.3.1 unchanged and create synchronized packet v2.4.0.
2. Reorder the proposal into the current seven-section structure: Identification, Images, Reference emoji,
   Factors for inclusion, Counterarguments to factors for exclusion, Other information, and Evidence of
   frequency.
3. Keep all required first-page administrative, image, and rights information at the top of page one.
4. Add a reproducible CLDR 48.2 English annotation audit. Record that `stomach`, `digestion`, `gut`, `abdomen`,
   and `nausea` have no exact annotation match, while related searches route to several different faces, foods,
   utensils, or pregnancy-related person emoji.
5. Use the CLDR result as evidence of an existing emoji search and semantic-routing gap. Do not claim that
   every product implements CLDR identically.
6. Keep Compatibility marked `N/A` because no documented high-frequency proprietary stomach pictograph has
   been found in Snapchat, X, QQ, or another major system. Discuss the CLDR issue as interoperability evidence,
   not as a fabricated positive compatibility claim.
7. Rebuild and visually inspect the PDF, then score the final packet against the 100-point approval rubric.

## Semantic version decision

This is a substantive response and format change, so the packet advances by MINOR version from v2.3.1 to
v2.4.0. The project advances by MINOR version from 0.27.1 to 0.28.0. Approved artwork remains byte-identical.

## Boundaries

- Do not file or publicly host the packet without explicit authorization.
- Do not add petitions, social-media request counts, disease-burden advocacy, or unsupported platform usage.
- Do not state that Compatibility passes without a popular-system pictograph and high-frequency evidence.
- Do not conceal open eligibility, public-URL, official-form, or final-review gates in the internal score.
