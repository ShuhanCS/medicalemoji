This is an external proposal for Unicode's 2026 emoji intake. The revision will preserve the approved
paired-kidney artwork, submitter roster, and strongest CLDR argument while matching the live proposal
format published by Unicode on 2026-05-20.

## Required corrections from v2.4.0

1. Replace the suggested keyword `kidney test`, which repeats the proposed name, with `test`.
2. Present the five required frequency exhibits as the primary empirical usage evidence. Keep the
   substitution examples and practitioner testimony as contextual evidence, not frequency measurement.
3. Mark Compatibility `Not applicable` because the packet does not contain screenshots and high-frequency
   evidence from a popular existing system as required for a positive Compatibility factor.
4. Retain the CLDR `kidney`-to-BEANS mismatch and the annotation-only rebuttal under **Breaks new ground**
   and **Already representable**, where they directly address representation and user ambiguity.
5. Remove the Emoji 19.0 priorities and Monarch Butterfly process paragraph from the formal Compatibility
   factor. Retain that material in internal strategy/research records rather than treating it as evidence
   that Kidney qualifies for the 2026 Compatibility factor.

## Frequency evidence decision

The 2026 detailed instructions require screenshots for Google Search, Google Video Search, Google Trends Web,
Google Trends Image, and Google Books Ngram. They expressly require `elephant` in the Trends and Books tools.
The current packet contains all five screenshots, result counts for Search and Video, and elephant comparisons
for both Trends exhibits and Books.

An automated refresh was attempted on 2026-07-28, but Google returned its unusual-traffic/CAPTCHA page. Do not
replace the valid May/July evidence with an anti-bot page or invent results. Preserve the dated, reproducible
captures and keep refresh as a manual pre-filing option.
