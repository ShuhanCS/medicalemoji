# Final medical emoji document package plan

## Purpose

Prepare a Microsoft-ready L2 discussion document and complete, individually submittable Unicode emoji proposal PDFs. The L2 is a standards-maintenance request; it is not a request to encode a bundle of characters. Every individual proposal must stand on its own under the current Unicode Emoji proposal requirements.

## Work products

### A. Final L2 document

`docs/proposals/utc-health-category/health-coverage-maintenance-l2-final.md` plus rendered PDF.

The L2 will:

- recognize health-related emoji coverage as a coherent standards-maintenance issue;
- review organization across anatomy, diagnostics, laboratory medicine, medication, emergency care, and clinical equipment;
- establish a transparent prioritization framework;
- route each individual concept through the normal Emoji proposal process; and
- define bounded vendor support for design review, accessibility, rights readiness, evidence reproducibility, and anticipated implementation.

It will not request automatic encoding, a complete anatomy taxonomy, a new Unicode category as a precondition, or preferential treatment for any single concept.

### B. Individual proposal packets

Each proposal packet will be a self-contained public PDF with its own:

- required first-page images: color and black-and-white at 18x18 and 72x72;
- submitter, date, name, keywords, category, and contact information;
- inclusion and exclusion factors;
- public frequency evidence and screenshot appendix;
- 18x18 legibility and accessibility discussion;
- design paradigm and vendor-variation guidance;
- image ownership, assignment, license, and provenance statement;
- coalition/support references used only as context, never as frequency evidence;
- eligibility and prior-submission check; and
- submission-form readiness checklist.

### C. Finalization sequence

1. Freeze the L2 scope and terminology.
2. Convert the coverage matrix into a concept-by-concept intake table.
3. Complete the six current proposal holds: evidence captures, image/right clearance, eligibility/date verification, submitter/contact confirmation, design sign-off, and final PDF/public-hosting checks.
4. Apply the same Unicode rubric to Kidney, Liver, Stomach, Pill Box, Inhaler, First Aid Kit, Blood Bag, IV Bag, Leg Cast, Weight Scale, EKG/ECG, and White Blood Cell as evidence supports.
5. Produce one final PDF per concept, with the evidence and design material inside that PDF rather than in a separate dossier.
6. Run a final legal, reproducibility, and visual review before submission.

## Quality gate

No packet is labeled SUBMIT or linked to the Unicode form until it contains no TODO, placeholder, or pending-capture language; all five required frequency sources are captured or a documented reproducible substitute is justified; all images have a rights chain; and the first page passes the 18x18/72x72 visual test.

## Versioning decision

This plan governs documentation and proposal artifacts. It does not require an application semantic-version bump; the repository version remains unchanged unless proposal tooling or release metadata is modified.
