# Kidney Emoji 100 Percent Readiness Plan - 2026-05-13

## Goal

Define and begin executing the work required to move the kidney emoji proposal from preliminary draft to a submission-ready public PDF with the strongest realistic chance of Unicode review.

## Definition Of 100 Percent Ready

The proposal is 100 percent ready only when:

- Unicode process eligibility is confirmed.
- TKF/ISN coordination is resolved and there is one aligned submission path.
- Final submitters, point of contact, and organizational roles are approved.
- Required proposal images are final at 18x18 and 72x72 in color and black-and-white.
- Image rights and license language are final and compatible with Unicode's required agreement.
- Frequency screenshots are captured and inserted into the proposal.
- All health/statistical claims cite current, stable sources.
- Support letters are linked or appended as coalition material only.
- The public PDF is hosted at a stable URL.
- The official Unicode form is ready to submit.
