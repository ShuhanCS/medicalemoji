# Stomach v2.3.0 Modern Artwork Integration Plan

Date: 2026-07-28

## Audience and outcome

The packet is for Unicode Emoji Standard & Research reviewers evaluating a standalone Stomach proposal. The
outcome is a self-contained `submissions/v2.3.0/` packet that uses the approved GPT Image 2 Stomach artwork
and preserves the completed v1.13 candidate's proposal, evidence, authorship, and rights records.

## Scope

1. Keep the existing Kidney `submissions/v2.2.0/` packet immutable.
2. Promote the completed `1.13.0-candidate.3` Stomach proposal as a separate v2.3.0 packet.
3. Carry forward the exact approved 18x18 and 72x72 color and true black-and-white assets.
4. Include the approved 1024x1024 source image as reference-only provenance.
5. Carry forward the proposal PDF byte-for-byte because its exact hash already passed page-by-page QA.
6. Rename packet files for v2.3.0 and update the Markdown's relative asset links without changing its claims.
7. Record exact source, asset, proposal, and PDF hashes in the packet manifest.
8. Render the promoted PDF and visually verify all pages before committing.

## Boundaries

- Stomach remains a standalone proposal; do not combine its prose, evidence, artwork, or form data with Kidney.
- Promotion does not authorize public hosting or filing through Unicode's form.
- Any change to one of the four approved submission images requires a new artwork decision.
- The v1.13 candidate PDF must remain byte-identical during promotion; a rebuilt PDF would require renewed QA.

## Semantic version decision

The repository's latest immutable packet is Kidney v2.2.0. Adding the separate Stomach proposal changes the
prepared submission scope and adds a complete proposal attachment set, so the synchronized packet advances by
MINOR version to `v2.3.0`. The project release advances from tag `v0.26.0` to `0.27.0`.
