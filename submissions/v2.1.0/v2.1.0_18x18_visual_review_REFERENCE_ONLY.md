## Review Result

Status: deterministic format gates passed; human recognition testing remains required.

The v2.1.0 assets use a native 18x18 single-kidney shape with a visible medial hilum and attached descending ureter. This gives the candidate one dominant silhouette at 18x18 rather than two small red forms separated by a vessel column. The artwork retains at least one transparent pixel between its 18x18 alpha bounding box and each canvas edge.

The review board includes an actual 18px comparison strip, color light/dark background checks, black-and-white white-tile checks, native 72px previews, bean-like and generic paired-organ comparators, and explicit candidate/risk labels.

The color 18x18 image has a flat organ-red body and tan urinary-system cue. The black-and-white 18x18 image uses a solid black kidney mass, white hilum landmark, and black ureter with a white rim. Fully opaque B&W pixels are strictly black or white; no opaque grayscale values remain. These are deterministic observations, not a claim of independent recognition.

## Recommendation

Use the current single-kidney candidate for the next review round. Before final public PDF submission:

- Ask at least 30 independent adults what the 18x18 and 72x72 color and black-and-white images depict without telling them the answer.
- Record first answer, forced-choice answer, and reported identifying cue; do not replace a cold-recognition result with design-team opinion.
- Require 80% correct color-18 recognition, 70% correct B&W-18 recognition, 90% correct 72px recognition, and 75% forced-choice Kidney selection for each 18px mode.
- Confirm that the visual review board remains readable in the exported public PDF.
- Do not claim the kidney shape is as universally iconic as a heart; frame it as a testable, sufficiently distinct organ paradigm.
