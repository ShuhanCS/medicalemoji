## Kidney

The `.6` color and black-and-white 72x72 examples are exact-size exports from polished GPT Image 2 masters
created for this project under Shuhan He's direction. The high-resolution masters are preserved in the package
and in `docs/design/kidney-emoji-2026-07/`. The submitted examples contain no copied third-party artwork,
logo, trademark, text, or vendor-specific emoji design.

The purpose-built 18x18 color and black-and-white assets remain byte-identical to `.5`. A mechanical reduction
of the detailed 72px concept was rejected because it weakened small-size separation from Lungs. The retained
18px pair preserves the stronger diagonal offset and passes the fixed technical comparisons. The legacy SVGs
remain as historical construction references; the `.6` master PNGs are the sources for the 72px exports.
