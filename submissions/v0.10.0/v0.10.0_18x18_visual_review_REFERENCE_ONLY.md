## Review Result

Status: acceptable for expert review, not final signoff.

The color 18x18 image remains distinguishable as paired kidney anatomy when shown next to a bean-like comparator. The central red/blue vessel contrast helps prevent the image from reading as a single bean. The paired-organ layout is the strongest visual cue.

The black-and-white 18x18 image remains a paired-organ shape, but the vessel and ureter detail collapses. This is expected at 18x18, but it means the final proposal should not rely on fine anatomical detail. The final argument should continue to emphasize global kidney function and use context as well as visual legibility.

## Recommendation

Use the current owned artwork for the next review round. Before final public PDF submission:

- Ask at least three non-nephrology reviewers what the 18x18 color image appears to show.
- Ask at least one Unicode/process reviewer whether the black-and-white image is acceptable.
- Keep the paired-organ design rather than switching to a single kidney silhouette.
- Do not claim the kidney shape is as universally iconic as a heart; frame it as recognizable enough when paired with the global functional case.
