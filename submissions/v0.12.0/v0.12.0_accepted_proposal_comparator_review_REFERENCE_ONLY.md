## Patterns Applied In v0.12.0

1. The proposal now leads the multiple-concepts section with everyday kidney uses: body, school science, kidney beans, kidney-shaped objects, hydration, filtration, wellness, family updates, donation, transplant, stones, urine, and health.
2. Public-health burden is still included, but it supports durability rather than carrying the whole proposal.
3. The image section now defines essential visual cues: paired organ form, curved kidney shape, medial indentation, and vessels or ureters.
4. The image section explicitly allows vendor variation in color, simplification, anatomical realism, and paired-organ rendering.
5. The already-represented objection is now answered with a substitute-failure table instead of relying on narrative alone.
6. Support letters are framed as coalition appendix material, not expected-usage proof.
7. Frequency evidence is presented as five figures in the proposal source, while operational blockers remain outside the PDF-facing proposal.

## Remaining Gaps Against Accepted-Proposal Patterns

The proposal still falls short of accepted-proposal polish in two areas:

1. The final PDF has not been exported and hosted as a clean public Unicode submission document.
2. Final eligibility, TKF/ISN coordination, submitter list, and signatory authority still require approval before filing.

## Next Drafting Rules

- Keep the public PDF short enough that reviewers can see the Unicode criteria without reading project history.
- Keep the first page focused on title, submitters, date, images, rights, and identification.
- Keep support letters and project process material out of the main argument unless they are moved to a concise appendix.
- Do not claim the kidney image must match the Medical Emoji artwork exactly.
- Do not describe kidney as only a nephrology or disease-awareness symbol.
- Do not submit until the five evidence images are visually confirmed in the exported public PDF.
