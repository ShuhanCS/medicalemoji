## 2. Selection thesis

Liver names the organ in “my liver-test results” and “this medicine may affect my liver.” Test Tube and Pill +
Warning express actions but omit the organ; Liver is a reusable anatomical referent, not a test, disease,
procedure, or campaign.

| Observed phrase or context | Meaning | Nearest substitute and remaining problem |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MedlinePlus names “liver function tests” and says results can show how the liver is working or whether medicines are harming it. | The subject is a test result about a particular organ. | Test Tube can mean a laboratory test, but it does not identify which organ the result concerns. |

## 4. Nearest-substitute analysis

The strongest short substitutes are Test Tube for a test and Pill + Warning for a medicine risk. They express
the action or concern well. They cannot identify the liver, so messages about a liver-test result or medicine
affecting the liver lose the precise organ referent. Cut of Meat is a visual confuser, not a reliable semantic
substitute for the anatomical organ.

## 5. Priority and scope

Likely neighboring unencoded concepts include other internal organs and narrower liver-related tests,
conditions, and procedures. The boundary is not membership in an anatomy list. Liver is the whole-organ noun
inside the established phrases “liver function tests,” medicines “affect the liver,” and “liver transplant.”
The candidate encodes that reusable literal referent, not any particular result, disease, procedure, or
specialty. Another candidate would need its own observed phrases, substitute gap, evidence, and recognizable
image; this case supplies no automatic path for it.

## 7. Strongest decline case

The strongest decline argument is that a missing literal referent plus high word frequency may still be too
narrowly clinical to justify one of a small number of permanent emoji additions, and that many internal organs
could make a similar request. Evidence that Test Tube and Pill + Warning already communicate the central
messages adequately would confirm that objection. The current rebuttal is narrower: each substitute expresses
the surrounding action but drops the exact noun that makes the cited phrases specific, while Liver represents
the whole organ rather than a diagnosis or procedure. This is a selection judgment, not a compliance failure.

## 8. Visual identity

Essential cues at 18x18 are a broad low outline, a larger rounded side, a smaller tapered side, and a diagonal
dividing line. The optional gallbladder may help in color but cannot carry identity. Likely confusers are
Anatomical Heart, Lungs, Brain, Cut of Meat, Beans, a leaf, and a generic organ shape. The comparison board and
computer checks establish technical separation; the project record treats Shuhan He's approval of the exact
