## Liver changes

- Worldwide widest-range 2026 Web and Image Trends captures replace the U.S.-only 2020 captures.
- A 2026-07-21 Ngram capture validates the English 1500-2022, smoothing-3 comparison against `elephant`.
- Historical Search and Video captures remain clearly labeled because Google blocked fresh results on this
  network with an unusual-traffic interstitial.
- Both 18x18 variants preserve stronger lobe and gallbladder/notch cues; monochrome art is true black and white.
- Four comparison boards cover 18x18 and 72x72, color and black-and-white targets.
- Unsupported usage lists were removed. Multiple meanings, Use in sequences, Completeness, and Compatibility
  are `Not applicable`.
- `liver/RECOGNITION-PROTOCOL.md` prepares an unprompted human test and records no invented outcome.
