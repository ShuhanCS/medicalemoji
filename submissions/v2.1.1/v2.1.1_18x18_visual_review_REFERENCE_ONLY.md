## Review Result

Status: deterministic format gates passed; no separate participant study is required for packet readiness.

The v2.1.1 assets use a native 18x18 single-kidney shape with a visible medial hilum and attached descending ureter. This gives the candidate one dominant silhouette at 18x18 rather than two small red forms separated by a vessel column. The artwork retains at least one transparent pixel between its 18x18 alpha bounding box and each canvas edge.

The review board includes an actual 18px comparison strip, color light/dark background checks, black-and-white white-tile checks, native 72px previews, bean-like and generic paired-organ comparators, and explicit candidate/risk labels.

The color 18x18 image has a flat organ-red body and tan urinary-system cue. The black-and-white 18x18 image uses a solid black kidney mass, white hilum landmark, and black ureter with a white rim. Fully opaque B&W pixels are strictly black or white; no opaque grayscale values remain. These are deterministic observations, not a claim of independent recognition.

## Recommendation

Use the current single-kidney candidate for the packet. Confirm that the visual-review board remains readable in the exported public PDF, and do not claim the kidney shape is as universally iconic as a heart.

Optional supplemental feedback may be collected to guide a future artwork revision. If it is collected, record its scope and results accurately; do not present informal feedback as an independent study or imply testing that did not occur.
