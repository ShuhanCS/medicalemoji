# Kidney Support Letters — Reference Only

Role: `REFERENCE_ONLY`. **Not part of the Unicode PDF.**

These letters are a real institutional asset and are retained here. They are deliberately kept
out of the proposal body: this repository's proposal rules state that advocacy and awareness
language appears far more often in proposals that were declined, and that petitions and
campaign-style support are disallowed evidence. The expected-usage case rests on the frequency
exhibits and the substitute-failure analysis instead.

The Medical Emoji project has recovered public support materials from nephrology, patient-advocacy, and kidney-health organizations, including a 2026 multi-specialty letter from the American College of Emergency Physicians. They are supplemental professional context only. The expected-usage case rests on public frequency evidence and the communication analysis in this proposal, not on support letters, petitions, hashtags, or social-media requests.
