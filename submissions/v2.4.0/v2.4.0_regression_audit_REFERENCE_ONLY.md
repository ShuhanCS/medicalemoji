Unicode-facing PDF.

## Restored and strengthened

- `v2.2.2` verifies that the CLDR English annotation data routes `kidney` only to U+1FAD8 BEANS and
  uses that mismatch under Breaks New Ground and Compatibility. `v2.4.0` restores that primary-source
  evidence while retaining the stronger paired-art and use-case material from `v2.3.2`.
- The proposal now answers the annotation-only objection directly: removing `kidney` from Beans would
  stop the misleading association but would leave no kidney-organ pictograph.
- Compatibility now relies on CLDR's documented emoji-search model and avoids claiming behavior for a
  named application or operating system without a reproducible exhibit.
- The proposal links the argument to Unicode's published Emoji 19.0 priorities and the UTC #185 record
  that compatibility materially affected the 2025 recommendations.
