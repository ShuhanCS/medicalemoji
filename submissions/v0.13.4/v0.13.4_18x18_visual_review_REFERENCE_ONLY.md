## Review Result

Status: acceptable for expert review, not final signoff.

The black-and-white image assets were replaced for `v0.13.4` because the prior hand-drawn black-and-white assets did not work visually. The new black-and-white assets are derived from a GPT-generated reference image with paired kidney outlines, clear medial indentation, and simple ureter cues. They are opaque white-background images with black line art, so they display consistently in GitHub, markdown previews, image viewers, and exported PDFs.

The review board includes an actual 18px comparison strip, color light/dark background checks, black-and-white white-tile checks, native 72px previews, bean-like and generic paired-organ comparators, and explicit pass/risk labels.

The color 18x18 image remains distinguishable as paired kidney anatomy when shown next to a bean-like comparator. The central red/blue vessel contrast helps prevent the image from reading as a single bean. The paired-organ layout is the strongest visual cue.

The black-and-white 18x18 image is now intentionally simplified from the generated reference. It keeps the paired outline and central cue without dense vessel clutter or transparency dependency. Treat it as improved for draft review, with final outside signoff still needed after PDF export.

## Recommendation

Use the current owned artwork for the next review round. Before final public PDF submission:

- Ask at least three non-nephrology reviewers what the 18x18 color image appears to show.
- Ask at least one Unicode/process reviewer whether the updated black-and-white line-art image is acceptable.
- Confirm that the fixed visual review board remains readable in the exported public PDF.
- Keep the paired-organ design rather than switching to a single kidney silhouette.
- Do not claim the kidney shape is as universally iconic as a heart; frame it as recognizable enough when paired with the global functional case.
