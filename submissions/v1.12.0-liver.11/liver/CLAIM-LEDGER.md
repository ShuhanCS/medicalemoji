## Claims deliberately excluded

- No metaphorical, symbolic, nutritional-benefit, recipe-popularity, or worldwide-food-preference claim is made.
- Metabolism and bile are omitted because they do not improve the strongest supported communication argument.
- “Recurring messages,” “worldwide use,” and a “pronounced recent peak” are not claimed. The evidence measures
  terminology, search interest, and published-word frequency.
- No burden, prevalence, awareness, campaign, endorsement, fixed anatomy set, or popular-system compatibility
  claim is used.
