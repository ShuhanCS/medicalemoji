## 2. Selection thesis

Liver names a recognizable whole organ across anatomy and biology, food and cooking, and healthcare. Test Tube,
Pill + Warning, Duck, Fork and Knife, and Cut of Meat express surrounding ideas but omit the liver itself; the
proposal is for the organ, not a test, disease, procedure, recipe, or campaign.

## 4. Nearest-substitute analysis

The strongest short substitutes are Test Tube for a test, Pill + Warning for a medicine risk, Duck for the
animal, Fork and Knife for a meal, and Cut of Meat for meat generally. They express those surrounding ideas well.
None identifies liver, so anatomy, food, test-result, and medicine-effect messages lose their precise subject.

## 5. Priority and scope

Likely neighboring concepts include other internal organs and narrower liver-related tests, conditions,
procedures, and dishes. The boundary is not membership in an anatomy or food list. Liver is a recognizable
whole-organ noun used across anatomy, food, testing, medicine effects, and transplantation. The candidate does
not encode a particular result, disease, procedure, specialty, or recipe.

## 7. Strongest decline case

The strongest decline argument is that word frequency and documented uses still do not directly measure demand
for a liver pictograph, and many concrete nouns could make a similar missing-referent claim. The rebuttal is
candidate-specific: liver crosses anatomy, food, and healthcare; the closest emoji express only surrounding
ideas; and the broad asymmetrical organ remains visually distinct at emoji size. This is a selection judgment,
not a compliance failure.

## 8. Visual identity

Essential cues at 18x18 are one asymmetric wedge, a dominant rounded lobe, a smaller tapered lobe, a shallow
lower notch, and a short curved lobe mark. The nested gallbladder may help in color but cannot carry identity. Likely confusers are
Anatomical Heart, Lungs, Brain, Cut of Meat, Beans, a leaf, and a generic organ shape. The comparison board and
computer checks establish technical separation; the project record treats Shuhan He's approval of the exact
