## Liver

GPT Image 2 was used to explore three Liver silhouettes. Shuhan He selected Candidate A and instructed the
project to update the proposal from concept-board version `1.0.1`. The submitted artwork in

with an asymmetric wedge, a dominant rounded lobe, a tapered smaller lobe, a short curved division, and a
