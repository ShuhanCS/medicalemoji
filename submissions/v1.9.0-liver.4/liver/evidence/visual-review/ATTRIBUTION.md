# Visual-comparison asset attribution

The comparison boards use the Liver and Stomach proposal artwork from this package, plus three OpenMoji
reference glyphs for visual-confusion testing:

- Anatomical Heart, U+1FAC0:
  https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hfg-gmuend/openmoji/master/color/svg/1FAC0.svg
- Cut of Meat, U+1F969:
  https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hfg-gmuend/openmoji/master/color/svg/1F969.svg
- Beans, U+1FAD8:
  https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hfg-gmuend/openmoji/master/color/svg/1FAD8.svg
- Corresponding black artwork:
  https://github.com/hfg-gmuend/openmoji/tree/master/black/svg

OpenMoji artwork is licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

The generic-organ comparison is a deliberately nonspecific project-created shape used only as a confusion
target. It is not a proposed emoji.
