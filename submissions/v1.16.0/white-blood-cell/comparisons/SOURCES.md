# White Blood Cell comparison sources

Board date: 2026-07-26

The proposed White Blood Cell assets are original Medical Emoji artwork released under CC0 1.0. The generic
cell control is generated by `scripts/build_white_blood_cell_artwork.py`.

The Microbe, Drop of Blood, Soap, and Bubbles comparators are pinned OpenMoji color and black SVGs from commit:

`d05930b34516a0a3ff00aad0288ee05364cebd8b`
