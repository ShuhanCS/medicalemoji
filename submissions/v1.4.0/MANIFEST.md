## Portfolio recommendation

**Make Ultrasound the evidence-sprint lead and keep CT Scan from `v1.3.0` as the filing-ready fallback.**
Ultrasound is the most promising novel healthcare concept found in the current public record, has a recent
Google Books signal approximately 1.70 times `elephant`, and can be represented without a patient or finding.
It should replace CT Scan as the Microsoft lead only after the four missing Google screenshots confirm a
strong current signal and a Microsoft product/design review accepts the monitor-and-probe paradigm.

Maze is the stronger of the two remaining additions because it has broad nonmedical meanings and a durable
books signal. Its two previous declines lower the expected value of a third filing, so it should not displace
Ultrasound or CT Scan without materially better current Trends evidence.

First Aid Kit is visually excellent and broadly useful, but its books signal is much lower and Adhesive
Bandage, Medical Symbol, and Toolbox create a more difficult `Already represented` argument. Hold it unless
Search, Video, and Trends results are substantially stronger than its Ngram comparison suggests.
