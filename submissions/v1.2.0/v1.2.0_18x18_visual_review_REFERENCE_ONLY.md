## Review Result

Status: acceptable for expert review, not final signoff.

The v1.2.0 assets replace the paired illustration with one large kidney shape, a visible medial hilum, and a short urinary-system cue. This gives the candidate one dominant silhouette at 18x18 rather than two small red forms separated by a vessel column.

The review board includes an actual 18px comparison strip, color light/dark background checks, black-and-white white-tile checks, native 72px previews, bean-like and generic paired-organ comparators, and explicit candidate/risk labels.

The color 18x18 image has an organ-red body and tan urinary-system cue. The black-and-white 18x18 image keeps the outer contour, hilum, and one outlined cue without grayscale shading. These are candidate observations, not a claim of independent recognition.

## Recommendation

Use the current single-kidney candidate for the next review round. Before final public PDF submission:

- Ask at least 12 people, including at least 8 non-medical reviewers, what the 18x18 color and black-and-white images depict without telling them the answer.
- Record first answer and confidence; do not replace a cold-recognition result with design-team opinion.
- Ask at least one Unicode/process reviewer whether the updated black-and-white line-art image is acceptable.
- Confirm that the visual review board remains readable in the exported public PDF.
- Do not claim the kidney shape is as universally iconic as a heart; frame it as a testable, sufficiently distinct organ paradigm.
