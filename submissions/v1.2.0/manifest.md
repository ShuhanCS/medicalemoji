## Filing preflight

The proposal PDFs are complete and contain no placeholders. Before pressing Submit, the filer should still perform these external actions:

1. Ask Unicode through the appropriate procedural channel whether the four-year rule permits concepts declined on 2022-11-04 in the intake closing 2026-07-31. The public guidance does not define the controlling date or retroactivity.
2. If Google permits a clean manual session, replace the 2020 Search, Video, and Trends captures for Stomach and Liver with current captures and rebuild the PDFs. The archived captures satisfy the five evidence categories and are dated accurately, but a current snapshot is stronger.
3. Review the final PDFs and accept the Unicode Emoji Proposal Agreement and License as the individual submitter.
4. Submit each organ separately through the official form: https://forms.gle/6KSiYHrUdBkTMNaB8

Official guidance: https://www.unicode.org/emoji/proposals.html
