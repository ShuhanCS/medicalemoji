## Research disposition

- `kidney stone`, `kidney tests`, `kidney transplant`, living donation, and dialysis survived as distinct,
  sourced communication conventions.
- Hydration, medication review, caregiver, school-science, awareness, and general support claims were rejected
  because they did not add a Kidney-specific communicative job.
- Raw Books and phrase comparisons are retained as scoped concessions and priority diagnostics, not as proof of
  emoji demand or entitlement.
- Beans and Beans + Hospital are the strongest retained substitutes. Their advantages are stated before their
  residual ambiguity.
- K-C11 and K-C12 must be recalculated or reconfirmed if any final artwork asset changes. The `.6` PDF hash,
  pagination results, and links are recorded after the final build; any exact-hash panel claim remains pending.
